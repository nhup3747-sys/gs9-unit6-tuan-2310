Unit 6: Vietnamese Lifestyle: Then and Now · 40 câu · 35 phút · Trọng tâm: Động từ + V-ing / to V: enjoy, avoid, mind, keep, practise + V-ing; want, decide, hope, plan + to V; like/love/start/begin + cả hai; remember/stop/try/forget + V-ing ↔ to V

Ghi nhớ nhanh: + V-ing: enjoy, avoid, mind, keep, practise (She avoids eating fast food.)
+ to V: want, decide, hope, plan (Linh decided to study abroad.)
+ cả hai: like, love, start, begin (I love reading = I love to read.)
Đổi nghĩa: remember/forget/stop + V-ing = việc đã xảy ra / ngừng làm; + to V = việc cần làm / dừng lại để làm. Ví dụ: I remember playing marbles. / Remember to lock the door.
